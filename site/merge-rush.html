    How to play

    Merge by pressing a + between two pieces. That pair joins into one piece everywhere it appears on the shelf, so the shelf holds fewer tiles.

    Each merge costs a move. A merge that saves 3 or more tiles at once is free.

    Deal puts the next word on the shelf and gives you 1 more move. The word arrives with all your merges already applied, in the order you made them.

    The shelf holds 18 tiles. If the next word won't fit, merge to make room. When no merge can make room, the run ends.

    Clear the deck by fitting all eight words. Your best run is kept on this device.

    The idea behind the game

    Merges that carry over

    Each merge you make becomes a rule, and every later word is broken up by your rules in the order you made them. A merge that pays off now can also shrink words you haven't seen yet, but only if those words contain the same pairs.

    Byte pair encoding builds a vocabulary for language models the same way. It starts from single characters, repeatedly joins the most frequent neighboring pair in its training text, and keeps the merges as an ordered list. A new word is split into pieces by applying that list in order, so frequent pairs end up as single tokens and rare words fall back to shorter pieces.
