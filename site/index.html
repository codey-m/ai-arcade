AI Arcade

Short games built from MIT course activities. Each one opens with a goal, plays out as you move things, and explains the idea once you finish.

  • 01 Probability+SDA · Bayes' rule

    Beach Beeps

    A metal detector beeps over most buried coins, but bottle caps set it off too. At each spot on the beach, decide whether digging every beep will pay. Play6.3710.1x
  • 02 Deep Learning · Model capacity

    Recycling Day

    Build a small network from pieces and train it to sort cans from bottles. How few pieces will do? Play6.7960.1x
  • 03 Probability+SDA · Independence

    Backup Power

    The harbor needs its lamps lit so boats can find the way in. Wire lamps to backup generators so enough stay lit on 9 nights in 10, for as little as possible. Play6.3710.1x
  • 04 Deep Learning · Generalization

    Rover Route

    Plan a delivery rover's route from nine measurements taken in mist. You get four bends. Where does the hidden trail really turn? Play6.7960.1x
  • 05 Probability+SDA · Distributions and expected value

    Prize Wheel

    Twelve sectors share a fixed prize budget, so the average prize never moves. Reshape the wheel so a spin reaches the bar as often as possible. Play6.3710.2x
  • 06 Deep Learning · Training

    Downhill Racer

    Set the robot's move strength and carry-over so it reaches its charger within par, without getting stuck or bouncing past. Play6.7960.2x
  • 07 Deep Learning · Convolution and the receptive field

    Lookout Tower

    A tower of lookouts reads a marsh photo floor by floor. Stack just enough floors, and halve the lookouts often enough, to outline every bird for the census on the least battery. Play6.7960.3x
  • 08 Deep Learning · Class imbalance

    Photo Finish

    Save six rare concert moments. Can 97.5% AI accuracy still leave your album incomplete? Play6.7960.2x
  • 09 Deep Learning · Compression

    Echo Dancer

    A dancer performs in one city while an echo dances the routine in another. The link carries two numbers a frame. Which two tracks keep the dance? Play6.7960.4x
  • 10 Probability+SDA · Power of a test

    Wonder Fold

    A friend says a new paper-plane fold flies 1.5 m farther. Plan the quickest test that backs a real gain at least 80% of the time. Play6.3710.5x
  • 11 Deep Learning · Generation

    Next Note

    Three songbirds write tunes one note at a time. Set the randomness and how many likely notes to keep, so each tune escapes its riff without odd leaps. Play6.7960.5x
  • 12 CV+NLP · Word order

    Robot Theatre

    Write the play's twist, then watch the director stage Act 1 all over again. It counts words and ignores their order. Play6.4600.1x
